## 🏗️ Overview

**Law-AI** is an AI-powered legal assistant designed for the Indian legal system. Upload any legal document and get an instant **plain-Hindi summary**, **risk score**, **red flags**, and **IPC section mappings**. In courtrooms, the AI listens live and suggests real-time responses and objections.

---

## ✨ Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| 📄 **Document Upload** | Upload legal documents (PDF, text) for instant analysis |
| 📝 **Plain-Hindi Summary** | Complex legal jargon translated into simple Hindi |
| ⚠️ **Risk Score** | Automated risk assessment of your legal position |
| 🚩 **Red Flag Detection** | Identifies dangerous clauses and loopholes |
| ⚖️ **IPC Section Mapping** | Maps document content to relevant IPC sections |
| 🎙️ **Live Courtroom AI** | Listens to proceedings and suggests real-time objections |

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5** — Document structure & layout
- **CSS3** — Responsive styling & animations
- **JavaScript** — AI integration & dynamic features
- **AI API** — Natural language processing for legal documents

---

## 🎯 The Problem

> **80% of Indians can't afford a lawyer.**
>
> Legal documents are written in complex English legalese that the average citizen cannot understand. Going to court without representation puts you at a massive disadvantage.

## ✅ The Solution

Law-AI democratizes legal access by:
1. Breaking down complex legal documents into plain Hindi
2. Identifying risks before you sign anything
3. Providing real-time courtroom assistance
4. Mapping legal content to IPC sections automatically

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

```bash
git clone https://github.com/shvmmonk/Law-AI.git
cd Law-AI
```

Open `index.html` in your browser and upload a legal document to get started.
